# v1.0.0

First release of the rewritten theme. The visual work was redone from the fork
point against measurements taken on a live OpenWrt 25.12 router, rather than
carried forward as a trail of guesses. `.dev/docs/DESIGN-RULES.md` states the
twelve rules the theme is held to, each written so it can be checked by
measurement instead of by eye.

## Layout

- The content column no longer carries a 720px cap. On a 2560px viewport that
  left 384px of the available width unused while LuCI's own tables overflowed
  their container.
- An empty table of contents reserves no gutter. Pages with fewer than two
  sections build no TOC, and holding the space regardless cost another 304px.
- Tables wider than the content column scroll inside their own box, whether the
  view wraps its table in a div or drops it straight into the section. The
  latter was clipped by nobody: a 933px table in a 752px column spilled 181px
  to the right and slid under the fixed table of contents.

## Typography and tables

- `word-break: break-all` is gone everywhere. It split words at whatever
  character met the edge, which shredded non-latin text — "Статический" wrapped
  as "Статическ / ий". Replaced with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, which breaks
  only what genuinely does not fit.
- Rows whose cells wrap align every cell to the top. Single-line rows are
  unaffected.
- The DHCP leases table no longer clips its own buttons. Fixed layout handed
  the actions column 107px for a 113px button and left the table 5px wider than
  its box — a scrollbar with nothing to scroll.

## Table of contents

- Entries read "Система" rather than "СистемаСкрыть": LuCI puts a collapse
  toggle inside section headings, and the heading text was being taken whole.
- Sections LuCI renders but leaves empty, such as DSL on hardware that has
  none, are not listed. Their entry could not be scrolled to.
- Every entry is reachable. The highlight used to move only when a section's
  top edge climbed past the header, which on a page that scrolls less than its
  sections are tall can never happen — measured on the firewall page, it
  scrolls 470px while its second section starts at 796px. The fold now sits a
  third of the way down the viewport, and clicking an entry pins it.
- The panel stays in its gutter. It measured its position before the entry list
  existed, while the gutter was still hidden, and ended up against the far left
  of the window.

## Dropdowns

- LuCI's `.cbi-dropdown` and the theme's `.outline-select` share one positioner
  and both render in the top layer, so two dropdowns on one form no longer
  behave differently, and a list opened inside a modal is not clipped by the
  modal's overflow box.
- Panels follow their trigger per frame while open. Scroll and resize events
  alone left them behind, stranding a panel at the top of the window while its
  field sat mid-page.
- Panels flip above their trigger only when they genuinely do not fit below,
  decided from the panel's own height rather than a flat guess that used to
  drop them on top of their own field.
- The list is handed back to LuCI on close. LuCI swaps the `dropdown` and
  `preview` classes between its two lists, so marking one as a popover
  permanently also caught the closed-state value display, which then floated
  free of the page.
- Both widgets use the same chevron and the same 180° turn on open.

## Forms

- Controls in a form row share a width floor, so a column of them no longer
  starts at six different offsets.
- Field descriptions and native selects inside modals are handled immediately.
  LuCI mounts `#modal_overlay` on `document.body`, so they used to be fixed up
  only when an unrelated poll mutated the page behind the modal — visible as
  the description jumping columns after the modal opened.

## Lists

- Badges in list views and interface boxes take the width of their column
  instead of each sizing to its own content.
- Firewall zone pairs keep their width on narrow screens rather than shrinking
  below their own badges.
