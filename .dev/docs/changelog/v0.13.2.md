# v0.13.2

Everything here was verified against a live OpenWrt 25.12 router rather than
inferred. See `.dev/docs/DESIGN-RULES.md` for the rules being checked.

## Table of contents

- The fixed panel no longer renders against the far left of the window. It
  measured its left edge before the entry list existed, while the gutter was
  still hidden and therefore reported an edge of zero. A regression introduced
  in v0.13.1.
- Entries read "Система" rather than "СистемаСкрыть". LuCI puts a collapse
  toggle inside section headings, and taking the heading text whole glued its
  label onto every entry. Headings carrying no letters or digits are skipped,
  which removes the dead "-" entry.
- Scroll-spy reaches the end of the page. Sections sharing the last screenful
  never crossed the activation threshold, so the final entry was unreachable
  however far you scrolled, and on short pages the highlight never moved at
  all.

## Dropdowns

- `.cbi-dropdown` and `.outline-select` now share one positioner and both
  render in the top layer. Previously LuCI placed one and the theme placed the
  other, so two dropdowns on the same form behaved differently, and inside a
  modal the list was clipped by the modal's overflow box.
- Panels are clamped to the viewport, flip above the trigger when there is no
  room below, and follow scroll and resize.
- Opening a dropdown inside a modal no longer gives the modal a horizontal
  scrollbar.

## Modals

- The observers that restyle selects and relocate field descriptions now watch
  `document.body`. LuCI mounts `#modal_overlay` on body, so selects inside a
  modal kept their unstyled native look, and descriptions only moved into the
  label column once an unrelated poll mutated the page behind the modal —
  visible as the description jumping columns after the modal opened.
